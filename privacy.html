Privacy policy

rclone personal backup is a private tool used by one person, its owner, to back up their own files to their own Google Drive.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads and writes files in the owner's Google Drive in order to store backup copies. It does not access any other Google data.

Where data goes

Files travel only between the owner's computer and the owner's Google Drive. Nothing is sent to, stored by, or shared with the developer or any third party. No analytics, advertising or tracking are used.

Other users

The tool is not offered to anyone else. If anyone other than the owner were to sign in, it would only ever access that person's own Drive, on their own computer.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
